System Overview

A student-led hardware and programming society built to push the boundaries of mechanical creativity.

The TekNora Robotics Club at Good Shepherd Convent Kandy bridges the gap between software and the physical world. Through hands-on soldering, circuit building, structural design, and microcontroller programming, we turn imagination into functioning machinery.

Design

Learn 3D modeling, structural mechanics, and how to draft blueprints for complex robotic chassis.

Wire

Master circuit boards, sensors, actuators, and the hardware that brings static metal to life.

Code

Program microcontrollers like Arduino and Raspberry Pi to give your machines logic and autonomy.

Hardware Operations

Our core modules for training the next generation of engineers.

Soldering & Circuits

Hands-on workshops teaching the fundamentals of electronics, PCB design, and safe hardware assembly.

Arduino Programming

Deep dives into C++ based microcontroller logic, teaching machines how to read sensors and move motors.

Robo-Wars & Competitions

Internal and inter-school challenges where students pit their custom-built robots against each other in arenas.

Autonomous Systems

Exploring line-following, obstacle avoidance, and basic AI logic for self-navigating rovers.

3D Printing & CAD

Designing custom gears, brackets, and robotic shells using Computer-Aided Design software.

IoT Home Automation

Connecting hardware to the internet to build smart-home prototypes and remote-controlled mechanisms.

Command Structure

The leadership matrix powering the club.

Teacher-in-Charge

Overseeing operations, lab safety, and providing advanced technical guidance.

Club President

Directing club vision, coordinating workshops, and leading major projects.

Hardware Lead

Managing the inventory of motors, sensors, and overseeing physical robot assembly.

Software Lead

Guiding the programming curriculum and helping students debug their code.

Secretary

Handling documentation, meeting logs, and external school communications.

Treasurer

Managing the club budget, component purchasing, and funding allocation.
