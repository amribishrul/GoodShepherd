About Our IT Society

A student-led technology society built to inspire curiosity, creativity, and innovation.

The Good Shepherd Convent Kandy IT Society provides students with opportunities to explore the fast-changing world of technology. Through workshops, competitions, projects, and interactive sessions, the society encourages students to think creatively, solve real-world problems, and build confidence in digital skills.

Learn

Explore coding, AI, IoT, robotics, and digital tools through guided activities.

Create

Build creative projects, websites, prototypes, and innovative tech solutions.

Lead

Develop teamwork, communication, leadership, and problem-solving skills.
